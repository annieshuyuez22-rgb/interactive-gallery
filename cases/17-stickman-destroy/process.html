Refinement sequence

What changed

4 documented implementation or specification improvements.

  1. 01

    Webpage-to-tile pipeline

    A page image is captured or replaced by an offline fallback, split into 16px tiles and classified against the dominant background color.

  2. 02

    Destruction that inherits the source page

    Tiles have health, scorching, fire spread and area damage; debris uses the original webpage pixels with smoke, sparks and explosion particles.

  3. 03

    Character and payload controls

    Running, jumping, flips, sustained flight, slam collisions, three viewport-clearing payloads and a bouncing timed grenade are implemented.

  4. 04

    Feedback and budgets

    Smooth follow, explosion shake, destruction HUD, cooldowns, pause/restart, sound and dark-mode controls are capped by a 1,300-particle budget and a 2× pixel-ratio limit.
