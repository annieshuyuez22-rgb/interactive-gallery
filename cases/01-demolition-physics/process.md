# Demolition Physics · Improvement archive

**Evidence level:** Prompt evolution — Basic → Advanced specification refinement

## Starting point

A single self-contained 2D Canvas demolition scene with a crane, breakable bricks, gravity, rotation, friction, dust and an automatic loop.

## What changed

1. **Scope expands to three 3D scenes** — The advanced brief adds a tornado and an overloaded truck collapsing a truss bridge, while allowing rendering and physics libraries.

2. **Structural failure becomes explicit** — A six-story bonded building, constrained chain, local bond failure, crack propagation and persistent rubble replace the generic collapse description.

3. **Materials and controls become testable** — Low-poly art direction, brick chips, chain tension, recoil, Pause/Reset, speed controls and three named camera presets are specified.
