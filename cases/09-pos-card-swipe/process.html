Prompt evolution

Starting point

A minimal isometric SVG card swipe with a moving card, glowing chip, receipt, success check and smooth easing.

Evidence standard: Basic → Advanced specification refinement

Refinement sequence

What changed

3 documented implementation or specification improvements.

  1. 01

    Geometry and material layers are specified

    Thirty-degree isometry, separate terminal faces, screen, keypad, reader slot, print port, embossed card details and ground shadow are added.

  2. 02

    Occlusion becomes a correctness requirement

    Inserted card sections must be clipped by the reader edge, the chip glows only during the swipe, and the receipt respects the print-port layer order.

  3. 03

    The transaction becomes an eight-second sequence

    Swipe, confirmation pulse, receipt expansion, stamped check, hold, card return and receipt reset must not overlap or jump at the loop boundary.
