# Pelican Cyclist · SVG · Prompt archive

## Basic prompt

```text
Create code for an SVG of a pelican riding a bicycle as nicely as you can
```

## Advanced prompt

```text
Create the complete code for a standalone SVG of a pelican riding a bicycle. Use only SVG elements, vector paths, gradients, clipping, and embedded CSS or native SVG animation; no raster images, external assets, canvas, or HTML wrapper. Give the SVG a responsive viewBox and an accessible title and description.

Show the whole bicycle and bird in a clean side view, with enough space around the long beak and both wheels. The pelican must be anatomically recognizable: cream-white plumage, layered charcoal flight feathers, a long amber bill, a distinct expandable throat pouch, a small alert eye, and broad orange webbed feet. Seat its body above the saddle, reach both feet to opposite pedals, and curl one wing toward the handlebar with a believable balance pose.

Build a teal diamond-frame bicycle with two equal circular wheels, slim black tires, silver spokes, hubs, fork, saddle, pedals, crank, and a visible chain connecting crank and rear sprocket. Keep the frame geometry coherent, with pedals below the saddle and the fork attached to the front wheel. Use warm coral accents and a pale sky-blue background with a simple ground line and soft elliptical contact shadows.

Animate a seamless riding loop: wheels and spokes rotate consistently with forward travel, the crank turns, webbed feet remain attached to their respective pedals, and jointed legs follow the pedal cycle. Add a small alternating body bob, a subtle pouch sway, an occasional blink, and sparse roadside flowers sliding past. Keep the pelican centered while the scenery moves.

Use crisp outlines with controlled line weights, restrained gradients on the bill and metal, and overlapping feather shapes that remain readable at small size. Eliminate detached feet, intersecting wheel geometry, accidental gaps, and motion jumps. Provide a still, fully composed pose under prefers-reduced-motion. Return only the SVG code.
```
