Refinement sequence

What changed

5 documented implementation or specification improvements.

  1. 01

    Rocket geometry and materials

    The richer path adds PBR environment reflections, seams, hatches and a shaped engine bell rather than relying on a simple Phong rocket.

  2. 02

    Ignition and heat

    A two-layer flame becomes a white-hot core, middle and outer plume with glow and simulated heat disturbance.

  3. 03

    Smoke and exhaust

    A single radial smoke pool becomes two recycling particle pools, with large smoke moving sideways through the flame trench and a denser exhaust trail.

  4. 04

    Launch mechanism

    Clamp release, umbilical-arm retraction and the flame trench turn launch into a sequence of mechanical events.

  5. 05

    Camera and telemetry

    Pad, Aerial and Tracking presets add smooth transitions, while countdown, altitude, stage, day/night and pad lights make the sequence easier to read.

Measured details

  • 3 camera presets in the richer implementation
  • 2 recycling smoke pools plus a separate exhaust trail
