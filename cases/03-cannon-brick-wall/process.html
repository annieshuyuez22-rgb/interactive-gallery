Cannon & Brick Wall · 大炮打砖墙

Final presentation

Prompt evolution

Starting point

Three brief Canvas scenes: a cannon demolishing a brick wall, bowling pins and a tornado.

Evidence standard: Basic → Advanced specification refinement

Refinement sequence

What changed

3 documented implementation or specification improvements.

  1. 01

    Physics must drive the outcome

    Independent HTML files, stable timesteps, restitution, friction, angular motion and projectile tunneling prevention are added; canned destruction is forbidden.

  2. 02

    Scene requirements become concrete

    At least 120 cannon-wall bricks, standard ten-pin arrangement and at least 40 tornado objects with different masses are specified.

  3. 03

    Controls and feedback are expanded

    Aim/power, two throws per frame, tornado steering, automatic demos, Pause/Reset and numeric controls join smoke, dust, chips and reflections.

Versions & evidence

Evidence boundary

This entry documents specification refinement from Basic to Advanced. It does not prove that every requirement was implemented or verified, and it is not presented as a complete code iteration log.
