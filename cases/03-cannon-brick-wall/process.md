# Cannon & Brick Wall · Improvement archive

**Evidence level:** Prompt evolution — Basic → Advanced specification refinement

## Starting point

Three brief Canvas scenes: a cannon demolishing a brick wall, bowling pins and a tornado.

## What changed

1. **Physics must drive the outcome** — Independent HTML files, stable timesteps, restitution, friction, angular motion and projectile tunneling prevention are added; canned destruction is forbidden.

2. **Scene requirements become concrete** — At least 120 cannon-wall bricks, standard ten-pin arrangement and at least 40 tornado objects with different masses are specified.

3. **Controls and feedback are expanded** — Aim/power, two throws per frame, tornado steering, automatic demos, Pause/Reset and numeric controls join smoke, dust, chips and reflections.
