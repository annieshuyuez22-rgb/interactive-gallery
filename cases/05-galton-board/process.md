# Galton Board · Improvement archive

**Evidence level:** Prompt evolution — Basic → Advanced specification refinement

## Starting point

A single-file Galton board with about 12 peg rows, collision-driven distribution, colored balls, bins, a histogram and pause/speed controls.

## What changed

1. **Collision and counting become stricter** — Twelve rows map to thirteen bins, with substeps, non-overlapping settled stacks and counting only after a ball settles.

2. **Trajectories and data become easier to read** — Peg highlights, contact shadows, hit flashes, recent-ball trails, exact bin counts and a shared scale are added.

3. **Interaction and performance become measurable** — Reset, Single Drop, 1–20 balls/second, active/settled/total counts and measured FPS are required, with sleeping settled balls retained.
