# Roman Colosseum · Improvement archive

**Evidence level:** Prompt evolution — Basic → Advanced specification refinement

## Starting point

A playable third-person Colosseum game with attacks, sprint, parry, enemy AI, health/stamina, waves and an arena crowd.

## What changed

1. **Architecture and crowd scale up** — Three tiers of arches, radial stairs, independent seats and at least 12,000 instanced spectators are specified, with collision on arena obstacles.

2. **Combat timing and fairness are clarified** — Attack windup/active/recovery, range and timing checks, no hits through walls, parry punishment windows and enemy stamina management are added.

3. **Match flow and presentation become explicit** — Three winnable rounds with 1/2/3 opponents, victory/defeat states, persistent fallen enemies, camera damping and two lighting modes are required.
