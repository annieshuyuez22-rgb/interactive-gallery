Roman Colosseum · 古罗马斗兽场

Final presentation

Prompt evolution

Starting point

A playable third-person Colosseum game with attacks, sprint, parry, enemy AI, health/stamina, waves and an arena crowd.

Evidence standard: Basic → Advanced specification refinement

Refinement sequence

What changed

3 documented implementation or specification improvements.

  1. 01

    Architecture and crowd scale up

    Three tiers of arches, radial stairs, independent seats and at least 12,000 instanced spectators are specified, with collision on arena obstacles.

  2. 02

    Combat timing and fairness are clarified

    Attack windup/active/recovery, range and timing checks, no hits through walls, parry punishment windows and enemy stamina management are added.

  3. 03

    Match flow and presentation become explicit

    Three winnable rounds with 1/2/3 opponents, victory/defeat states, persistent fallen enemies, camera damping and two lighting modes are required.
